# Bass Glossary

Canonical language for this course. A term is added only once Frank can use it correctly.

## Terms

**Root motion**:
The sequence of root notes (the bass's job) following the song's chord changes. The bass anchors each chord on its root and walks from chord to chord.
_Avoid_: bassline outline, chord roots

**Signature hook**:
The short, recognizable bass figure that an audience knows and sings — the part that makes a cover sound like "the actual song."
_Avoid_: riff (too vague), the catchy bit

**The hard part**:
The single 4-8 bar section of a song that gets looped first, at slow tempo, until it stops being the bottleneck.
_Avoid_: tricky section, weak spot

**Pocket**:
Playing in time with the drums such that the groove sits in the same rhythmic space; locked in, not rushing or dragging.
_Avoid_: groove (reserved for the general feel)

**Tempo ramp**:
Starting a section at 60-70% of target tempo and adding 2-3 bpm per day until it reaches full speed.
_Avoid_: metronome build-up

## Musical shorthand

- **i–VI–III–VII** — roman numerals for scale degrees (lowercase = minor, uppercase = major), e.g. Am–F–C–G in A minor.
- **Pentatonic** — a five-note scale used for fills; E minor pentatonic covers most of this setlist.
