# Mission: Become Gig-Ready on Bass

## Why
Frank has a reunion gig with the G6 band on Friday, October 9, 2026 at Canyon Lake. After roughly a year away from the instrument, the goal is to walk on stage able to play the full set from memory — comfortably, in time, and without charts or panic — and to genuinely enjoy it.

## Success looks like
- Playing all 7 core songs (plus 2 backups) through from memory at gig tempo.
- Hitting the recognizable signature hooks, not just the root motion.
- Locking with Paul (guitar/vocals) and Fidel in a full-band run before the gig.
- Walking off stage with no "I panicked and lost the form" moment.

## Constraints
- Deadline: October 9, 2026 — roughly 7 weeks.
- Returning player (played ~a year ago; relearning after unpacking the gear).
- Uses Ultimate Guitar (paid) for full tabs/playback — the course owns the *bass-specific* gold (root motion, hooks, hard parts), not full transcriptions.
- Practice in ~45-60 min daily sessions; deliberate rest Oct 7-8.

## Out of scope
- Note-for-note transcription fidelity (fills/embellishment are stretch, not required).
- General music theory beyond what a song needs ("one transferable idea" per lesson).
- Gig logistics / load-in / setlist planning — that lives in the LogSeq vault, not this public course.
