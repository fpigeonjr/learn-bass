Dancing Queen

ABBA · key of A · target ~100 bpm

New song — learn it cold. The octave disco line is the whole trick.

1 · Form map

Intro → Verse → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro

2 · Root motion

The verse is a walking pattern through A – D – A – E. The chorus lifts to a bigger A – F♯m – D – E feel.

Verse: A D A E E |--5--------------------------------7--- A |--0--------5--------0--------7----------

3 · Signature hooks

ABBA bass is all about the octave disco line — root, then the octave above, driving straight eighths.

A octave disco: G |--------| D |--------| A |--0-0------------------| E |------0-0--2-2--4-4--5-5-- (root octave root octave...)

Once you can grab the octave shape without looking, this song unlocks fast.

4 · Tempo ramp

Target ~100 bpm. Start at 65 bpm and add 2-3 bpm per day. The octave jumps are the constraint, not the tempo.

5 · The hard part

The octave disco line in the verse and chorus — loop it until the octave shape is automatic and even.

6 · Feel

Straight eighths, bright and bouncy — this is the opposite of the DeLeo swing. Fingers, and keep the octave jumps snappy, not flabby.

7 · Memory cue

"Root, octave, root, octave." The form is a pop standard: verse twice, chorus, verse, chorus, bridge, chorus, out.

8 · Technique primer

Warm up with the A major scale and a few octave-shape reps across the neck (5 min).
