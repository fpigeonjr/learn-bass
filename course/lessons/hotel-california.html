Hotel California

Eagles · key of B minor · target ~75 bpm

1 · Form map

Intro → Verse → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Guitar solo → Outro

2 · Root motion

B minor. The classic loop: Bm – F♯ – A – E – G – D – Em – F♯. This is the signature.

Bm F♯ A E G D Em F♯ E |--7----2----5----7----3----5----7----2--- A |--7----4----5----7----5----5----7----4---

3 · Signature hook

The descending intro arpeggio and the climbing bass under the verses. The root-motion loop is the backbone the whole song hangs on.

4 · Tempo ramp

Target ~75 bpm. Slow and easy — but the arpeggio accuracy is the test. Start at 55 bpm.

5 · The hard part

The intro arpeggio + the verse root-motion — smooth, legato, in the pocket at the slow tempo.

6 · Feel

Fingers, legato and smooth. This one breathes — don't rush, let the slow tempo open up.

7 · Memory cue

"Bm–F♯–A–E–G–D–Em–F♯, repeat." Know the loop and the form is automatic.

8 · Technique primer

Warm up with B minor and its arpeggio — the intro line is chord tones.
