Interstate Love Song

Stone Temple Pilots · key of A minor · target ~89 bpm

This is the hard song. Start now — it gets the most time, earliest.

1 · Form map

Intro → Verse → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro

The verse is the signature groove. The chorus lifts. The intro guitar riff (Paul) sets the pocket you sit inside.

2 · Root motion

The whole song cycles a four-chord loop: Am – F – C – G (i–VI–III–VII in A minor).

Am F C G E |--0----1----3----3-- A |--0----3----3----3-- (roots on the low strings)

Keep the roots even and driving. This is a DeLeo line — the motion between chords matters as much as the chords.

3 · Signature hooks

The recognizable part is the verse groove — a steady, slightly swung eighth-note pulse over the Am–F–C–G loop, with the unison run into the chorus.

Verse (swung 8ths, root-heavy): G |----------------| D |----------------| A |--0-0-0--3-3-3--3-3-3--3-3-3--| E |----------------| Am F C G

4 · Tempo ramp

Target ~89 bpm. Start at 55 bpm (just over 60%) and add 2-3 bpm per day. Groove first, speed later.

5 · The hard part

The verse root-motion + the unison run into the chorus. Loop those 8 bars in isolation until the swing feels locked and the transition is seamless.

6 · Feel

Fingers (not pick), slightly behind the beat — DeLeo sits in the pocket, not on top. Keep the swung feel relaxed, never rushed.

7 · Memory cue

"Four chords, forever" — Am–F–C–G never changes. If you know the loop, you know the song; the sections are just how many times you play it.

8 · Technique primer

Warm up with the A minor scale and the Am arpeggio (5 min) before starting — this song is all chord tones from A minor.
