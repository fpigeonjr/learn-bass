Orientation

Why this course exists, and how one song becomes green.

The mission

Friday, October 9, 2026. Canyon Lake. The G6 band, back together. We have roughly seven weeks to walk on stage and play the whole set — from memory, in time, and without panic. The setlist lives on the dashboard.

What "green" means

A song is green when you can play, from memory, at gig tempo:

  • the root motion through every section,
  • the groove locked in time,
  • the signature hooks people recognize.

Fills and embellishment are the cherry on top — not the bar.

The daily session (45-60 min)

  • 0-5 — stretch + spider warm-up
  • 5-10 — right-hand alternation + muted groove
  • 10-20 — scales/arpeggios with metronome
  • 20-45 — song work: loop the hard part, slow metronome, build
  • 45-50 — cool-down: play something you love

How to use a song lesson

Every song lesson has the same eight parts:

  1. Form map — the sections and how they repeat.
  2. Root motion — the bass note through each section.
  3. Signature hooks — the recognizable parts, as tab.
  4. Tempo ramp — target BPM, start at 60-70%.
  5. The hard part — the 4-8 bars to loop first.
  6. Feel — finger vs pick, pocket, dynamics.
  7. Memory cue — how to remember the form.
  8. Technique primer — the scale/warm-up this song needs.

Slow down, don't rush. Record yourself weekly on your phone and listen back without judging. Play standing with a strap sometimes — that's the gig posture.
