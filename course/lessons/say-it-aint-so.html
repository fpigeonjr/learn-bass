Say It Ain't So

Weezer · key of G (verse) / C (chorus) · target ~77 bpm

1 · Form map

Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro

2 · Root motion

The intro hooks immediately — that climbing line is the signature. Verse sits on G, chorus moves to C.

Intro hook (climbing): G |--------------| D |----------0---| A |--------3-----| E |--0--2--3-----| G A B♭ B

3 · Signature hook

The intro chromatic climb (G → A → B♭ → B) is the part everyone knows. Nail it clean, then settle into the verse groove.

4 · Tempo ramp

Target ~77 bpm. Start at 55 bpm. This one's slow — the groove is the test, not speed.

5 · The hard part

The intro hook + verse root-motion. Loop the climb until it's clean, then work the verse pocket.

6 · Feel

Fingers, relaxed, slightly laid back. The verse is steady eighths; keep it even so the chorus lift has room.

7 · Memory cue

"Climb, then chill." Intro climbs, then a long even verse — easy to remember the shape.

8 · Technique primer

Warm up with G major and a chromatic walk (the intro is literally chromatic).
